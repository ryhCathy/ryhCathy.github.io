Hello! I’m a **Solution Architect at Amazon Web Services**, focused on **Automotive & GenAI**. I help OEMs, Tier-1s, and ISVs take AI workloads from PoC to production. <br>

My work centers on **LLM training & inference, GPU/Kubernetes (EKS) infrastructure, ADAS data loops, and agentic AI platforms** — sizing and autoscaling GPU clusters, optimizing serving (vLLM · SGLang · TensorRT-LLM · KV-cache), and turning customer requirements into reference architectures across automotive workloads (ADAS, connected mobility, smart cockpit, SDV). <br>

Before AWS, I earned an M.S. in Information Systems Management at **Carnegie Mellon University** (Highest Distinction) and a B.Sc. in Computing at **The Hong Kong Polytechnic University** (First-Class Honours), with exchanges at NUS and the University of Cambridge. <br>

If you’re working on GenAI or GPU infrastructure and want to compare notes, I’d love to connect.
